Offline corpus difficulty analysis
=================================

From the repository root:

  .venv/bin/python report/baseline_analysis/inspect_corpus_difficulty.py
  .venv/bin/python report/baseline_analysis/explain_corpus_difficulty.py
  .venv/bin/python report/baseline_analysis/pool_size_counterfactual.py
  .venv/bin/python report/baseline_analysis/validate_corpus_difficulty.py

The first script reads the Question 1 selected-report catalog and original saved
source tables, questions, qrels and rankings. It computes all 638 query features,
English corpus term frequencies, all-model dense controls and 21 seeded cases.
The second checks saved text/rankings for answer-bearing unjudged passages and
creates numeric controls, scientific figures (PNG/PDF/SVG) and index.html.
The third computes a uniform-pool-thinning expectation from saved English ranks,
with all evaluation sources forced to remain and bounds for top100 censoring.
None performs new retrieval, inference, generation, job submission or paid calls.

Open index.html for figures and full text cases. Read findings.txt for manually
reviewed interpretation. The seeded sample is not weighted to estimate prevalence;
the CSV features and diagnostics use their documented complete query cohorts.
Rank 101 in the feature files means censored beyond saved100, never exact rank101.

Tests (offline fixtures plus exact small-pool enumeration):

  .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q report/baseline_analysis/test_pool_size_counterfactual.py report/baseline_analysis/test_recall_at_10.py

The extraction and pool scripts record source hashes and methods. The final
manifest captures the delivered outputs and the manually authored findings.
If raw results change, regenerate the diagnostics and manually review the findings
again; fixed prose is a historical interpretation, not automatic validation of
changed inputs. English tokenization/stopwords are diagnostics, not the embedding
tokenizer, and are not applied as cross-language lexical comparators.

Dependencies: the baseline_analysis requirements plus pyarrow and scipy; exact
versions are recorded in environment.json. Prepared source texts are public
benchmark inputs already present locally; no network is required.
