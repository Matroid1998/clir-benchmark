The present invention relates to the development of insecticidal nanoemulsions containing plant or "green" active ingredients that significantly reduce the infestation of B cubenis, an insect that causes an irreversible damage to guanabana fruits, comprising the following manufacturing process: The citronella nanoemulsion (NE) is prepared with a continuous phase by dispersing glycerol (25%, w/w) in double deionized water; the dispersed phase is constructed by dissolving tween 80 (at 10%, v/v) in citronella essential oil. Both phases are mixed separately by magnetic stirring for 24 hours at 40°C in the dark. To formulate citronella NE, the dispersed phase (10% w/w) is homogenized in a continuous phase (90% w/w) by two high-energy methods, high-speed homogenization (Ultra-Turrax T25, IKA WERKE, Germany) and ultrasound (Vibra-Cell VC 505/VC750, USA) at 15,000 rpm/3 min, and 20 kHz/50% amplitude/5 min, respectively; The NE loaded with citronella-piperine. The continuous phase is constructed by dispersing glycerol (25% v/v) in double deionized water (DD H2O); the dispersed phase is constructed by dissolving Tween 80(10% v/v) in citronella essential oil. Each phase is magnetically stirred for 24 h at 40°C in the dark. To formulate NE with piperine is added at concentrations of 0.1% in the dispersed phase and dissolved by two high-energy methods, high-speed homogenization (Ultra Turrax T25, IKA-WERKE, Germany) and ultrasound (Vibra-Cell VC 505/VC750, USA) at 15,000 rpm/3 min, and 20 kHz/50% amplitude/5 min, respectively; The pyrethrin-loaded NE is prepared by the low energy method and the oil-in-water (O/W) type. Tween 80 and Span 80 surfactant are mixed with HLB: 13 in a proportion of 85: 15 with the composition of 7.1% (v/v), and water is added under magnetic stirring for 1 h until complete homogenization. Subsequently, 4.7% (w/w) pyrethrin is added to the mixture of surfactants and water in the presence of mechanical stirring using a 6000 rpm mixer for 2 h. Next, the nanoemulsion is stirred for 15 minutes in an Ultra-Turrax (r) at 10000 rpm. Finally, it is maintained in an ambar flask to prevent the photodegradation of pyrethrin; for NE loaded with azadirachtin, Toximul (HLB: 15) is used at 10% (w/w) as a surfactant, and the corresponding amount of azadirachtin 4% (w/w) is added slightly under magnetic stirring for 2 h until complete homogenization. Subsequently, deionized water is added to the Toximul/Azadirachtin mixture and mixed under magnetic stirring and after 15 minutes in Ultra-Turrax ® at 10,000 rpm. Finally, the nanoemulsion is packaged in an ambar container.
