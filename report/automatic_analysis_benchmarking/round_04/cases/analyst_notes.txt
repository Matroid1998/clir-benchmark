ROUND 4 QUALITATIVE CASE INSPECTION
Model-analyst observations, 2026-11-15. These are not independent human judgments,
paid grading results, or a translation-quality evaluation.

Selection and scope
The six cases were chosen by scripts/select_round04_cases.py before their texts
were inspected. The script, candidate-pool CSVs and selection_manifest.json retain
the rules, ordering, denominators, seed, complete selected query IDs and input
hashes. All six came from clear-quality pools, so no quality fallback was needed.
No original-query-language restriction was applied. Extreme cases illustrate
possibilities, not their frequency or average effect. The stable control uses a
seeded choice among 14 eligible questions, not the most visually persuasive case.

UN E5 clear-pool sizes: 39 English-hit/German-miss of 40 before quality restriction;
18 German-hit/English-miss of 18; 118 both-hit of 126; 14 stable controls of 14.
EUR-Lex and chemistry BGE examples maximize/minimize the same English-output,
mixed-setting nDCG contrast among 163 and 182 clear queries, respectively.

The three UN extreme cases selected under these rules happen to have English
originals. Their English translation is an exact identity, confirmed in the
saved generation record. This is not evidence from three independently generated
English paraphrases. The stable UN control and both BGE cases originate in French.
Ten/Tde below mean the language of the retrieval query, not the original language.

Full original questions, translations/HyDE, saved answers, source IDs, generator
provenance, qrels and ranking statistics are in the matching .txt/.json files.
JSON retains saved top-100 rank identities/scores and winning-chunk texts for
top-10 results and accepted positives within 100. The present observations were
made from those query texts, the first-ranked result, and the first accepted
positive; they do not certify every document's semantic relevance.

Saved answers/evidence were inspected here after case selection. They were not
inputs to query translation or HyDE generation. Empty saved evidence strings in
the legal examples are retained, not filled with invented source spans.

1. un_e5_source_loss
Query: q_4bac2def037911480b8b1ed3fb59d26d853ac5cb2392ea225a422ec71c854db1
Source: 2001/a/res/55/231, results-based budgeting.

Visible text: the German query retains the General Assembly, its resolution on
results-based budgeting, implementation of approved measures, and the request
for conditions. It adds no answer proposition. The inspected texts do not show
an obvious omitted date/entity or a contradiction. This observation is narrower
than certifying translation equivalence.

Ranks: Ten finds the English source first and only that accepted version in the
top ten; Tde first finds it at rank 17, so nDCG@10 changes .390380 to 0 and source
Hit changes 1 to 0. Ten's top ten are all English; Tde's are nine French and one
Spanish. The Tde top result is another resolution (68/241), and its displayed
chunk discusses financing and reporting on implementation. It is unaccepted
under the source qrels, not automatically semantically unrelated in every respect.

The winning chunk for the accepted English document also changes: Ten selects
the beginning containing the implementation conditions, while Tde selects a
later portion. Thus a different language expression coincides with different
winning chunks and ranks. The case does not identify why E5 changed its scores.

2. un_e5_source_gain
Query: q_ba28f17d8c78afc99c921a76c1c398976d692554faf8c180994d9f9e41483934
Source: 2011/a/res/65/99, adopted 10 December 2010.

Visible text: both query forms retain the reporting obligation, General Assembly,
Secretary-General, the date 10 December 2010, June 1967, and subsequent hostilities.
The German form asks the same kind of question; no answer-bearing deadline is
supplied in the query itself.

Ranks: Ten's accepted source is at 11; Tde retrieves its French version at 1 and
English version at 5, moving nDCG@10 from 0 to .541400. Ten's rank-one result is
resolution 57/119 on the same displacement topic from a different year. Its text
has closely related reporting language. The query's specified date makes source
identity relevant; the result should not be labeled wholly unrelated merely
because its qrel is zero. The French positive selected by Tde visibly includes
the reporting obligation and 10 December 2010 date. This is a concrete recovery
of the accepted dated source, not evidence of a general German advantage.

3. un_e5_version_gain_both_hit
Query: q_db24165b1a872216cc32f5ca1ec0bbd756e6170dd7adcfa40137eef1d815c121
Source: 2013/a/res/67/100, relations with the UN host country.

Visible text: the German query retains visa concerns, anticipated host-country
measures, the General Assembly, UN host-country relations, and 14 December 2012.
No particular visa decision or legal instrument is inserted as an answer.

Ranks: both forms already rank the accepted English source first. Ten retrieves
one accepted version in the top ten; Tde retrieves all four at ranks 1, 2, 5, 8
(English, French, Spanish, Chinese). Source Hit and MRR@10 remain 1, while nDCG
rises from .390380 to .910853. This is a direct illustration of additional-version
reward without improved first-source position for this question.

The English document's winning chunks differ. Ten's displayed chunk is the
resolution's concluding agenda/date portion; Tde's contains its earlier visa
provisions. Document-level qrels mark the document relevant in both cases, and
the metric does not certify answer-bearing quality of the winning chunk. This
additional observation cannot be generalized to answer quality without a separate
span or downstream evaluation.

4. un_e5_stable_control
Query: q_39f367930441d1d2bd68347bf8c89ccd8b6beb920c6f939b9157c615274d8ea4
Source: 2006/s/res/1698_2006_, Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Visible text: both translations preserve the request for an end date, the DRC,
the Security Council, 2006, and measures concerning child recruitment. Neither
inserts the saved answer, 31 July 2007. The source chunk visibly states that date.

Ranks: Ten and Tde both find the English source at 1 with one accepted version
in the top ten, giving identical .390380 nDCG, .25 version Recall and 1 Hit/MRR.
The original French question finds the French version at 3. Equal top-ten metrics
do not mean identical retrieval lists: Ten's top ten are all English, Tde's are
four English and six French. Tde also finds accepted versions at 27, 31 and 86,
outside the selected cutoff. The stable designation applies to the specified
top-ten scores/counts, not all ranks or every cutoff.

5. eurlex_bge_hyde_gain
Query: q_1586007449bc6cb14409b743f373bcd6ce4cec0af8ec9d68fe30c1b545241501
Source: Regulation 4/2009, Article 50.

Visible text: the English translation asks which network central authorities
handling maintenance obligations should use. HyDE supplies the name "European
Judicial Network in civil and commercial matters", which is present in the
saved answer and the accepted English article. This is observable addition of
an answer-matching named entity absent from the question. It is not proof that
the model saw the source at inference time; its generation input was the query
with fixed task/domain instructions. Pretraining exposure is not audited here.

HyDE also adds sentences about practical information exchange, contacts,
cross-border cases and procedures. The inspected short source article supports
cooperation and information exchange in general; this note does not individually
verify every added proposition or classify the additions as hallucinations.

Ranks: translation's first accepted source is 17; HyDE returns all four accepted
versions at 1-4, changing nDCG from 0 to 1. The translation's rank-one document
concerns cooperation and charging across rail networks, illustrating shared
network/cooperation vocabulary under the fixed qrels. No lexical ablation was
performed, so the named entity cannot be claimed as the isolated cause.

6. chemistry_bge_hyde_loss
Query: q_aed3759927fa7ca3a9b1fff822f8ff0ab3572f214b48e81e1e33985005dfc8ef
Source: patent WO-2019095513-A1, filter-core device and humidifier abstract.

Visible text: translation retains the upper housing, filter-core device,
inverted U-shape and location question about water inlets. HyDE retains those
concepts but converts the question to a passage describing a "transverse crown",
"depending side portions", flow toward the core, and an "upper transverse
portion". The saved answer/source describes the horizontal central part of the
inverted U-shaped housing. The source excerpt does not use the same English
terminology, and the added geometry/flow description is not established as false
by this inspection. Do not classify this as a proven factual error or hallucination.
At a broad spatial level, the proposed transverse upper location does not show
an obvious contradiction with the saved horizontal-central answer; this short
comparison is insufficient to certify the passage's complete faithfulness.

Ranks: translation returns the two accepted versions, Chinese and French, at
1-2. HyDE returns them at 28 and 19, respectively, changing nDCG from 1 to 0.
Its rank-one result is a different filter/membrane patent with tubes, covers and
water-flow terminology. Both variants have the same top-ten language counts
(7 English, 1 Spanish, 1 French, 1 Chinese), so this example's loss is not a
change in that marginal language distribution. Different identities occupy the
ranks. The example shows that retaining recognizable task concepts in a generated
passage is insufficient to guarantee better source retrieval; it does not isolate
length, word choice, factuality or model geometry as the cause.

Cross-case interpretation limits
The two BGE examples use identical empty query/document prefixes and the same
English output language. This avoids the prefix difference present for E5/Gemma/
Qwen/Nomic HyDE, but still compares different text lengths, structures and content.
The outcomes are selected extremes, so they do not estimate how often answer-
matching additions help or harmless-looking reformulations hurt. The UN examples
show gains, losses, unchanged source success with more versions, and equal cutoff
metrics with different lists. They support inspection of what changed; none
establishes a German bridge mechanism or a causal explanation for corpus means.
