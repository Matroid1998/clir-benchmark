Generation input contract

The saved experiment request builders supply the original question text and
language metadata, together with fixed translation/HyDE instructions. HyDE also
supplies the collection name and its fixed domain/style prompt. They do not pass
the saved answer, evidence spans, source-document text, retrieved passages or
candidate score to the generation request.

This is a source-code/input-contract audit. It does not establish absence of
pretraining exposure to public source documents, or independently grade the
faithfulness or factuality of any generated output. The original questions are
themselves source-derived synthetic questions, as disclosed in the study scope.
